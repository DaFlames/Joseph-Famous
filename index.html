Hi There! I'm Joseph Famous

Computer Engineer, a Cybersecurity student at Miva Open University, and the founder of Arxia Core.

I don’t just love building the future—I'm dedicated to securing it.

Brief Biography

My journey into tech started with a childhood fascination with robotics, heavily inspired by the inventions of Iron Man. I knew early on that I wanted to build complex, intelligent machines. Thanks to a supportive foundation, I jumped into the field early—completing a year of intensive Computer Engineering training in 2020, followed by years of hands-on industry work both on-site and remotely while pursuing my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering in Northern Cyprus.

While my university years opened my eyes to the incredible scope of engineering, my true turning point came during my senior year. A conversation about the vulnerability of robotics made me realize a critical truth: building advanced technology is pointless if it can be weaponized by others.

Determined to bridge the gap between physical hardware and digital defense, I earned certifications from ISC2 and Cisco NetAcad, and am currently deep-ening my expertise in Cybersecurity at Miva Open University. To bring this intersection of hardware engineering and advanced security to the real world, I founded Arxia Core, a startup focusing on IT Infrastructure and Cybersecurity. I will always be a robotics engineer at heart, but today, my mission is protecting the individuals, corporations, and governments utilizing the tech of tomorrow.
